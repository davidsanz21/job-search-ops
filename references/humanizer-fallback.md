# Humanizer fallback (used only when the humanAIzer skill is not installed)

Apply to every outward draft, silently, in two passes. The full companion skill (`companion-skills/humanAIzer/`) is stronger — prefer it when installed.

## Pass 1 — mechanical cleanup

**Vocabulary:** replace inflated verbs (leverage→use, utilize→use, delve→get into, foster→build, facilitate→help, streamline→simplify) and hollow adjectives (robust, seamless, cutting-edge, comprehensive, innovative, pivotal) with plain words or specifics. Cut dead metaphors (landscape, tapestry, ecosystem, testament, cornerstone) and filler ("It is worth noting", "In today's fast-paced world", "At the end of the day", "In conclusion").

**Structure:**
- Kill the negation flourish ("It's not X — it's Y") → commit to a direct statement.
- Break mechanical rules-of-three; use two items, or four, or one.
- Vary sentence length on purpose; uniform rhythm reads machine-made.
- No false balance ("While X, also Y") in persuasive documents — take the position.
- No signposting ("Let me explain", "Here's what you need to know") — just say it.
- No sycophancy or collaborative artifacts ("I hope this helps").
- No generic positive endings ("exciting times ahead") — end on something specific or a concrete ask.
- Em dashes only where the pause changes meaning; otherwise comma or period.
- Bullet lists with bold labels → prose, unless the document type demands bullets (CVs do).
- Active voice; name who does what.
- No vague attributions ("experts say") — name it or cut it.

## Pass 2 — the self-audit

Reread and ask: *"What still makes this obviously AI-generated?"* List the remaining tells; fix exactly those. In CVs and letters specifically check for: adjective stacking without evidence, identical bullet openers, and summaries that could describe anyone.

## Voice

If the user has provided writing samples, match their sentence-length pattern, punctuation habits, and recurring phrasings. If not: direct, varied rhythm, first person in letters, zero corporate filler.
